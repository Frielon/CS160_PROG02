No API key

The AI parts of the guide need an OpenRouter key. Copy js/config.local.example.js to js/config.local.js, paste your key into OPENROUTER_API_KEY, and reload.

Without one you can still read the certified text, the printed arguments, the topic counts and what the data pack says about each office: none of those call a model.

Compare the two sides

The guide prints four documents for every measure: an argument for it, an argument against it, and one rebuttal from each side. Pick a measure to see what each side claims, side by side, and how the two sides answered each other. An AI model pulls out the claims; our code checks every quote against the printed text. Nothing here says which side is right.

The offices at the bottom of your ballot

These five seats decide real things that most voters can’t name. Pick one to see exactly what our data pack says it decides. An AI model can explain that in everyday words, including what the office does not decide, and draw a picture of something it affects. Everything the AI writes or draws is labeled as AI-generated.
